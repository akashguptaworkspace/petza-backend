Request Account Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of a Petza or Petza Partner account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email from your registered email address, or include your registered phone number, so we can verify the account before deletion.

Email Deletion Request

What Will Be Deleted

We will delete or anonymize your account profile, authentication records, saved app data, listings where removal is allowed, uploaded media associated with deleted listings, and chat or enquiry data where deletion is legally and operationally permitted.

What May Be Retained

We may retain records required for legal, fraud-prevention, safety, dispute, tax, payment, or compliance reasons. Data retained for these purposes is limited and retained only as long as necessary.

In-App Path

In Petza, open Profile and choose Delete Account. In Petza Partner, open More and choose Delete Account.
