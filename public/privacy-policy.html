Petza Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This policy explains how Petza and Petza Partner collect and use data for pet marketplace, partner onboarding, listing, enquiry, chat, and account features.

Information We Collect

We collect account identifiers such as email address or phone number, profile details you provide, authentication data, pet listing details, store or business profile details, KYC/onboarding information, uploaded media, enquiry messages, and optional location information when you grant location permission.

How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts, show pet and store listings, support partner dashboards, process enquiries and chat messages, prevent abuse, provide support, and improve app reliability.

Sharing

Listing and business profile information may be visible to other users. Chat messages are shared with the other party in the conversation. We may use service providers for hosting, email, SMS, storage, analytics, or payment processing when those services are enabled.

Location and Media

Location access is optional and used to show nearby pets, stores, and services. Uploaded photos, videos, and documents are used for listings, verification, or support workflows depending on where you upload them.

Security and Retention

We use HTTPS and access controls to protect data in transit and in our systems. We retain information while your account is active or as needed for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, and operational purposes.

Account Deletion

You can request account and associated data deletion from inside the app or at /account-deletion.html.

Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, email privacy@petza.vedantaa.in.
